# References

Primary methods and criteria used to draft this working protocol. Verify PDFs against the lab’s access before citing in manuscripts.

1. **Pozzobon M, Saggioro M, D’Agostino S, Bisogno G, Muraca M, Gamba P.** Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma decellularization. *Methods Mol Biol.* 2017. doi:[10.1007/7651_2017_45](https://doi.org/10.1007/7651_2017_45) — ARMS-specific DET scaffold chapter.

2. **Sgro A.** *Tissue engineering for the surgical treatment of muscle defects…* PhD thesis, University of Padova. Describes DET cycles (water 24 h → detergent 4 h → washes → DNase 3 h), **1% SDS for ARMS**, **4% SDC for muscle**, DNA/collagen/GAG assays, and freezing medium. [Padua Research Archive PDF](http://paduaresearch.cab.unipd.it/10570/1/Sgro_Alberto_Tesi.pdf)

3. **Crapo PM, Gilbert TW, Badylak SF.** An overview of tissue and whole organ decellularization processes. *Biomaterials.* 2011;32(12):3233–3243. doi:[10.1016/j.biomaterials.2011.01.057](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2011.01.057) — Minimal criteria: <50 ng dsDNA/mg dry ECM; <200 bp fragments; no visible nuclei on DAPI/H&E.

4. **Piccoli M et al.** Related detergent–enzymatic muscle/diaphragm scaffold work cited by the Padua group (orthotopic diaphragmatic ECM). Use when tracing the DET lineage. **CONFIRM** exact Piccoli 2016 citation before manuscript use.

5. **Almins et al. / comparative skeletal muscle reviews** (e.g., Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology 2022, “Decellularization Strategies for Regenerating Cardiac and Skeletal Muscle Tissues”; standardized SDS vs Triton/trypsin vs SDC comparisons) — guidance that SDS often balances DNA removal and mechanics on thick muscle better than SDC alone.

6. **Chronopoulos A, Chavez I, Vemula CK, et al.** The PAX3-FOXO1 fusion gene reduces cell–ECM interactions and TGFβ signaling in rhabdomyosarcoma. *J Cell Biol.* 2025. — Lab context for why native ECM scaffolds matter for fusion-positive vs fusion-negative phenotypes.

7. **Hurdles in DNA QC:** reports that silica columns can lose <200 bp fragments and that DAPI autofluorescence can mislead — prefer Qubit on lysates and consider Feulgen when needed.

This folder is a **draft lab protocol**, not a copy of any single publisher’s full Methods text. For submission or SOP formalization, rewrite in CHLA format and cite the primary papers directly.
