# Skeletal muscle decellularization (4% SDC DET)

Same DET cycle timing as ARMS; detergent is **4% sodium deoxycholate** (Padua muscle / diaphragm immersion workflow). Use this as the healthy-muscle comparator scaffold.

## One DET cycle (~31 h)

| Step | Solution | Condition | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hypotonic lysis | Sterile deionized water | 4 °C | **24 h** |
| 2. Detergent | **4% SDC** | Room temperature, gentle agitation | **4 h** |
| 3. Detergent washout | Sterile deionized water | **3–4** changes | Until detergent cleared |
| 4. DNA clearance | **2000 kU DNase I in 1 M NaCl** | Room temperature, gentle agitation | **3 h** |

Repeat for **2–3 cycles**, then ≥ 3 days PBS + 3% P/S rinse with daily changes.

## SDS alternative for thick muscle

Comparative skeletal-muscle studies often find **1% SDS** clears DNA more reliably than SDC alone on thick samples, with better cytocompatibility after washout, while prolonged trypsin / Triton combinations can damage mechanics.

If 4% SDC leaves residual nuclei after 3 cycles on your thickness:

1. Prefer **thinner slices** first.
2. If still incomplete, run a parallel arm with **1% SDS** using the ARMS timing (document which detergent each batch received).
3. Do not mix SDC and SDS in the same bath without a dedicated wash between; keep arms separate for interpretation.

**CONFIRM** which detergent becomes the lab standard for muscle before publishing or multi-week campaigns.

## Architecture note

Muscle ECM should retain fascicular / aligned cues when possible. Avoid mincing if the experiment needs oriented topography for RMS cell spreading or migration; use intact slices of defined thickness instead.
