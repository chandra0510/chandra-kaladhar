# Tissue harvest

## ARMS xenograft (fusion-positive scaffold source)

Published Padua workflow used RH30 xenografts in immunodeficient mice (Balb/c Rag2−/− γc−/−). **CONFIRM** which fusion-positive line and host strain are approved under the current Park-lab IACUC protocol (RH30, Rh41, or another PAX3-FOXO1 line already in use).

1. Grow subcutaneous flank tumors to the size approved in the protocol (avoid necrotic cores when possible; large necrotic centers leave debris that resists clearance).
2. Euthanize per approved method.
3. Excise tumor aseptically. Trim skin, fat, and grossly necrotic regions.
4. Rinse briefly in sterile cold 1× PBS.
5. Cut into pieces of uniform thickness — target **≤ ~3–5 mm** in the shortest dimension so detergent reaches the core. Record wet weight.
6. Optional snapshot: fix a native piece for histology / DNA / collagen / GAG baselines from the **same** tumor.
7. Keep remaining pieces in sterile PBS on ice and start DET the same day.

Do not freeze before the first DET cycle unless a freeze–thaw arm is an intentional experimental variable (freeze–thaw can aid lysis but also damage ultrastructure).

## Healthy skeletal muscle (comparator)

1. Harvest quadriceps or another named muscle from the approved donor strain/age (**CONFIRM** species: murine vs other).
2. Rinse in sterile 1× PBS; remove fascia and tendon as needed for the experiment.
3. Bisect or slice to the same thickness convention as tumor pieces.
4. Reserve a native matched piece for QC baselines.
5. Proceed to the muscle DET protocol the same day.

## Labeling

Label every piece with: tissue type (ARMS vs muscle), animal/tumor ID, harvest date, intended detergent (SDS vs SDC), and target number of DET cycles. Keep a batch sheet ([CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md)).
