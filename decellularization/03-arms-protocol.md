# ARMS decellularization (1% SDS DET)

Adapted from the detergent–enzymatic ARMS protocol (Pozzobon et al., 2017; Sgro DET description: same cycle structure with **1% SDS** substituted for SDC).

Work sterile after the first water step if scaffolds will be reseeded. Change solutions by pouring off into chemical waste and replacing; do not crush the tissue with forceps.

## One DET cycle (~31 h)

Bath volume example: **40 mL** per piece (scale so tissue moves freely).

| Step | Solution | Condition | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hypotonic lysis | Sterile deionized water | 4 °C, static or very gentle rock | **24 h** |
| 2. Detergent | **1% SDS** | Room temperature, gentle agitation | **4 h** |
| 3. Detergent washout | Sterile deionized water | Room temperature; **3–4** complete changes | Until foam/detergent largely gone (typically 30–60 min total) |
| 4. DNA clearance | **2000 kU DNase I in 1 M NaCl** | Room temperature, gentle agitation | **3 h** |

Washout after SDS is mandatory: residual SDS inhibits DNase and is cytotoxic to cells seeded later.

## Number of cycles

Run **2–3 DET cycles** for ARMS pieces of a few millimeters. Check nuclear clearance (DAPI on a sacrificial slice) after cycle 2.

- If nuclei remain dense in the core → add cycle 3 (or thin the pieces next batch).
- If the matrix is already friable and DNA is below threshold after cycle 2 → stop; further SDS will strip GAGs and weaken mechanics.

**CONFIRM** the cycle number locked for production after the first QC-qualified batch.

## After the final cycle

1. Rinse scaffolds **≥ 3 days** in sterile 1× PBS + 3% P/S at 4 °C, changing buffer daily. This clears residual detergent and enzyme.
2. **Optional PAA:** after detergent is fully washed out, treat with **0.1% peracetic acid + 4% ethanol for 1–2 h**, then extensive sterile PBS washes — see [06-storage-and-reseeding.md](06-storage-and-reseeding.md). Do not add PAA into the SDS bath.
3. Split the batch:
   - **QC aliquot** — fix / digest for DNA, histology, collagen, GAG (see [05-quality-control.md](05-quality-control.md))
   - **Storage aliquot** — freezing medium or short-term PBS+antibiotics (see [06-storage-and-reseeding.md](06-storage-and-reseeding.md))
   - **Experiment aliquot** — proceed to sterilization (PAA if used) and reseeding

## Expected gross appearance

Successful ARMS scaffolds typically become pale / translucent relative to native tumor and hold a cohesive 3D shape. Complete disintegration means detergent exposure or agitation was too aggressive for that thickness.
