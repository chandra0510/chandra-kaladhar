# How to submit

## The opportunity

The Rhabdo Coalition Collaborative Research Grant uses one application and one Scientific Advisory Board review. Participating nonprofits then decide separately whether to fund a recommended project, and at what amount. A strong review does not guarantee funding.

- Portal: https://grants.rhabdocoalition.org/
- Register, then create the application. The username is your email address.
- Program contact: grants@rhabdocoalition.org
- Application opened: October 17, 2026
- Deadline: **November 30, 2026, 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time** (8:59 p.m. Pacific Time)
- Scientific Advisory Board review: December 1, 2026 – January 30, 2027
- Funding decisions expected by February 15, 2027
- Anticipated start: March 1, 2027

Save the application as final in the portal, then download a copy. Applicants receive confirmation that it was received.
