## Rules

- Do not add fold-changes, p-values, concentrations, or sample sizes that are not on a figure.
- Do not describe SPOCK3 knockout, expansion microscopy of SPOCK3-null cells, or finished atomic force measurements as completed work. Those are proposed Aim 1 and Aim 2 experiments.
- If cortical tension has only been inferred and not measured, keep “appears” in the significance and approach sections.
- If a claim held in the fellowship text but failed on repeat, delete it from every portal section, not only from the approach.
