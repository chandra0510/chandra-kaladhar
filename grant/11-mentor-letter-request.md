# What the mentor letter needs to say

The portal asks for letters from collaborators or consultants who have a real role. For this application the essential letter is from JinSeok Park, PhD. Send him this list. The letter should be on CHLA letterhead, signed, and limited to what he can attest. Do not draft sentences for him to sign unchanged if they overstate effort or funding.

Ask the letter to confirm:

1. He is Dr. Vemula’s mentor and Dr. Vemula will lead the proposed experiments in his laboratory.
2. The laboratory has the fusion-positive spheroid models, the PAX3-FOXO1-knockdown system, and the confocal reflection microscopy assay the approach uses, including Rh41.
3. The percent effort and the statement that the rest of Dr. Vemula’s salary and the base laboratory operation come from non-overlapping sources. He should name those sources only if Sponsored Projects agrees they do not overlap this project.
4. He will meet regularly with Dr. Vemula on experimental design, image-analysis decisions, and interpretation, and he will review the data deposit and the manuscript.
5. CHLA will provide the bench space and microscope access the project needs.
6. He supports submission to the Rhabdo Coalition Collaborative Research Grant for a March 1, 2027 start.

If Sangyoon J. Han, PhD, will analyze new imaging data, ask him for a separate letter that states that role and nothing broader. If he will not, do not list him as a collaborator.

Attach Dr. Park’s current NIH biosketch (five pages) with the letter.
