# What the mentor letter needs to say

The portal asks for letters from collaborators or consultants who have a real role. For this application the essential letter is from JinSeok Park, PhD (Assistant Professor of Pediatrics, Keck School of Medicine of USC; Investigator, Cancer and Blood Disease Institute, CHLA). Send him this list. The letter should be on CHLA letterhead, signed, and limited to what he can attest. Do not draft sentences for him to sign unchanged if they overstate effort or funding.

Ask the letter to confirm:

1. He is Dr. Vemula’s mentor and Dr. Vemula will lead the proposed SPOCK3 experiments in his laboratory.
2. The laboratory has the fusion-positive rhabdomyosarcoma models and the PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown system this project uses. He should also state where atomic force microscopy and expansion microscopy will be done, if he can attest to that access.
3. The percent effort and the statement that the rest of Dr. Vemula’s salary and the base laboratory operation come from non-overlapping sources. He should name those sources only if Sponsored Projects agrees they do not overlap this project. In particular, confirm that the National Pediatric Cancer Foundation metastatic-rhabdomyosarcoma award and the Alex’s Lemonade Stand Foundation A Award do not already budget the SPOCK3 knockout, expansion microscopy, surfen, atomic force microscopy, or narrow-pore work requested here.
4. He will meet regularly with Dr. Vemula on experimental design, image-analysis decisions, and interpretation, and he will review the data deposit and the manuscript.
5. CHLA will provide the bench space and microscope access the project needs.
6. He supports submission to the Rhabdo Coalition Collaborative Research Grant for a March 1, 2027 start.

If someone outside the laboratory will run atomic force microscopy, ask that person for a separate letter that states that role and nothing broader. If the work stays inside the Park laboratory, do not add a collaborator.

Attach Dr. Park’s current NIH biosketch (five pages) with the letter.
