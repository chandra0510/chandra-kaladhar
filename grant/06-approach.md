# Approach

## Strategy

Both aims use fusion-positive rhabdomyosarcoma cells already growing in this laboratory, including the PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown system used for the preliminary SPOCK3 observations. A fusion-negative line already in the laboratory is the comparison that lacks the fusion. Parental fusion-positive cells, PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown cells, and new SPOCK3 knockout cells are compared side by side, so a SPOCK3 result can be read against the phenotype that started the project. No mice and no patient specimens are required.

SPOCK3 will be knocked out with CRISPR-Cas9. Guides, the parental line, and an empty-vector or non-targeting control will be documented. Knockout will be confirmed at the DNA and at SPOCK3 protein or transcript before any phenotype is interpreted. If protein detection is not reliable, loss of transcript plus the heparan sulfate phenotype will be the working confirmation, and that limit will be stated. Surfen is the pharmacologic arm on the same endpoints. It is interpreted only together with the knockout, because a small-molecule heparan sulfate antagonist can have effects beyond SPOCK3.

## Preliminary data

Knockdown of PAX3-FOXO1 reduces SPOCK3 and reduces heparan sulfate available to capture growth factor. Those cells capture less FGF2, show less FGF2-driven ERK activation, and proliferate less. Cortical tension appears lower after knockdown, and the cells are less able to pass through narrow pores, used here as an in vitro indication of extravasation. These statements match the preliminary results already described for the Summer’s Way submission. **CONFIRM** each one against the current figures before paste. If a result has not held, delete it rather than carry it forward.

The published work from this laboratory is separate. It showed that PAX3-FOXO1 raises NOS1 and nitric oxide, suppresses TGFβ signaling, and reduces cell–matrix interaction. That study supplies the fusion-positive models and the knockdown system. It did not identify SPOCK3, and this application does not claim that it did.

## Aim 1. SPOCK3, FGF2 capture, and proliferation

Months 1–10. After SPOCK3 knockout is validated, expansion microscopy will measure heparan sulfate abundance and bound FGF2 at a resolution conventional light microscopy does not provide. The expansion factor will be measured in each experiment so a brighter or larger signal is not mistaken for a biological change. A conventional heparan sulfate stain on unexpanded cells will be run in parallel. FGF2 capture will be compared in parental cells, PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown cells, SPOCK3 knockout cells, and surfen-treated parental cells.

Proliferation will be read by CCK-8 and by live-cell imaging over the same conditions. ERK activation after FGF2 will be read by a biochemical assay already available in the laboratory, or by a phospho-ERK immunoblot if that is the assay in hand. The prediction is that SPOCK3 knockout reduces FGF2 capture, ERK activation, and proliferation, in the same direction as PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown and as surfen. If knockout does not, and surfen does, Aim 1 concludes that heparan sulfate matters but SPOCK3 is not the required proteoglycan.

Pitfalls. Other proteoglycans may compensate after SPOCK3 loss. Total heparan sulfate staining is included so compensation is visible. Expansion can distort the cell surface; the unexpanded stain is the check. Surfen can act off target. A surfen effect without a knockout effect will not be called a SPOCK3 result.

## Aim 2. Cortical tension and pore passage

Months 8–20. Cortical tension will be measured by atomic force microscopy on the same genotypes and on surfen-treated cells. Indentation conditions will be fixed before the groups are compared, and each cell is a technical observation inside a biological replicate of independently plated cultures. **CONFIRM** where the atomic force microscope sits and who trains the operator. If instrument time is not available, Aim 2 still completes the pore assay, and tension is reported as not measured rather than replaced by an untested method.

Passage through narrow pores will be measured by transwell assay. Pore size will be small enough that passage requires deformation, and a larger pore will be the control for motility that does not require that deformation. The prediction is that SPOCK3 knockout and surfen lower cortical tension and reduce passage through the narrow pore, in the same direction as PAX3-FOXO1 knockdown. Reduced passage through both narrow and large pores will be interpreted as a general drop in motility, not as an extravasation-specific result.

The transwell is a model of squeezing through a pore. It is not a blood vessel, and this proposal does not call it metastasis in an animal. A negative tension or pore result is a completed aim. It would mean the preliminary knockdown phenotype does not trace through SPOCK3.

## Close-out

Months 1–4: generate and validate the SPOCK3 knockout. Months 4–10: expansion microscopy, FGF2 capture, ERK, and proliferation, with surfen. Months 8–18: atomic force microscopy and transwell assays. Months 18–24: deposit any new molecular data, post the protocols, send the Coalition the plain-language report, and draft the manuscript. The one-year minimum budget stops at the end of Aim 1 and does not include the atomic-force and transwell campaign.

This application will not start xenografts, screen a new chemical series, or claim surfen as a clinical candidate. Those steps are appropriate only if Aim 1 or Aim 2 shows that SPOCK3 or heparan sulfate is required for the phenotype.
