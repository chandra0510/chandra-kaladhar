## Rhabdomyosarcoma relevance

Limit: 150 words. Draft: 134 words.

This project studies only fusion-positive rhabdomyosarcoma, the subtype driven by PAX3-FOXO1 and the group of children and young adults most likely to relapse with metastatic disease. The question is how that fusion builds a WEE1-high follower population that supports collective invasion, and whether this cell state explains both the invasive behavior of these tumors and the weak clinical activity of the WEE1 inhibitor adavosertib in relapsed rhabdomyosarcoma. WEE1 is the therapeutic target. The models are three-dimensional spheroids of fusion-positive cells, including Rh41, in which this laboratory already measures matrix interaction, cell-cycle state, and invasion. Fusion-negative cells are used only as the comparison that lacks PAX3-FOXO1. The work is aimed at a decision families and investigators still do not have: when a WEE1 drug is worth pursuing in this disease, and when it is not.

## Data, resource sharing, and collaboration

Limit: 200 words. Draft: 161 words.

This award will produce imaging data, quantified invasion measurements, and, if new sequencing is required to link PAX3-FOXO1 to cell-cycle genes, the related RNA or chromatin profiles. New sequencing data will be deposited in the Gene Expression Omnibus when the paper is published or within 12 months after the award ends, whichever comes first. PAX3-FOXO1-knockdown RNA-seq already generated in the laboratory will be deposited on the same schedule if it is not already public. Processed invasion measurements and the notes required to reproduce the leader–follower comparisons will be released with the paper. The hanging-drop spheroid method and the confocal reflection microscopy assay used to measure matrix remodeling will be posted as step-by-step protocols. Reporter lines and related constructs will be shared with academic rhabdomyosarcoma laboratories under a Children’s Hospital Los Angeles material-transfer agreement. Each year we will give the Rhabdo Coalition a short, nontechnical update that member organizations can share with families. This project does not generate individual patient-level data.

## Funding leverage and impact

Limit: 150 words. Draft: 121 words.

Coalition funding is what turns a preliminary observation into a finished test of WEE1 in fusion-positive invasion. The laboratory already has the fusion-positive models, the matrix-imaging assays, and early evidence that WEE1 blockade slows three-dimensional growth and invasion. Dedicated support is not in place to map leader and follower cells, to test whether the drug effect depends on PAX3-FOXO1 level, or to set those results beside the negative pediatric trial. This award would pay for that work over two years and for the time to deposit the data and explain it in plain language. Without it, the WEE1 result remains a meeting abstract. With it, the field gets a direct answer on when WEE1 blockade is worth pursuing in fusion-positive rhabdomyosarcoma.
