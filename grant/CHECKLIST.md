## After you submit

The Scientific Advisory Board reviews from December 1, 2026 through January 30, 2027. Each nonprofit then decides independently whether to fund. Decisions are expected by February 15, 2027. The anticipated start is March 1, 2027.

If the project is funded, one combined scientific report replaces separate reports to each funder. It must cover progress, findings, changes in approach, outputs, use of funds, next steps, and a short quote the nonprofits may use with their donors. Publications and talks must acknowledge the Rhabdo Coalition and the specific funders named in the award agreement.
